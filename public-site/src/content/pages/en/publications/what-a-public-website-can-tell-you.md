## The public surface is evidence, not the whole story

A public review can identify whether a visitor has a visible route from question to service, a way to contact a person, and enough explanatory material to judge fit. It cannot substitute for analytics, user research or a client's private commercial context.

## A responsible research rule

Keep structural observations small and explicit. Treat interpretations as hypotheses. Do not turn public examples into training material unless the appropriate owner has given consent and a human has reviewed the decision.
