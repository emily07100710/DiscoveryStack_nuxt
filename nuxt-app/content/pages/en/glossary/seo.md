## SEO in one sentence

SEO is the practice of helping a useful page be found and understood when someone searches for a relevant need.

## What a service website needs

It needs a page for a real intent, a clear explanation, accessible HTML, sensible internal links and enough evidence for a reader to evaluate the promise. No single tactic replaces the system.
