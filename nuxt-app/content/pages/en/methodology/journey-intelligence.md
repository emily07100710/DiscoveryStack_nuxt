## We do not start by guessing what is wrong.

Journey Intelligence begins with a simple distinction: an observed fact is not the same thing as an interpretation. A missing contact path can be observed. Why a specific visitor left cannot be asserted from a public page alone.

## The review sequence

1. **Observe.** Record visible structural signals, such as whether a page has a primary call to action, service routing, a visible contact path, FAQ or evidence of a clear next step.
2. **Frame.** Describe the likely friction as a hypothesis with its supporting evidence.
3. **Prioritize.** Select the smallest useful change that could reduce uncertainty or improve movement.
4. **Review.** Keep decisions that affect client strategy, training data or commercial claims under human review.
