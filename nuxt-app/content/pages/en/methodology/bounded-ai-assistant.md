## Public answers need a boundary.

The assistant can explain the approved service scope, the review process and the difference between public observation and a strategic conclusion. It cannot promise ranking, invent a project outcome, disclose private examples or substitute for a scoped human conversation.

## A good handoff is not a failure

When a question requires business context, confidential data or a recommendation with commercial consequences, the correct answer is to make the next human conversation easier—not to guess.
